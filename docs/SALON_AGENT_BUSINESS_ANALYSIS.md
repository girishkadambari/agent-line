# AgentLine — Complete Business Analysis
### Source: Customer interview (27th Main Road, Bangalore) + Market context

---

## THE ONE-LINE TRUTH

> A solo salon owner loses 25–35% of monthly revenue not because his work is bad — but because his phone rings while his hands are cutting hair, his regulars assume he's busy and stop calling, and nobody tells walk-ins what's available. AgentLine is the 24/7 business partner that fixes all three — by phone, by WhatsApp, automatically.

---

## PART 1 — WHAT THE INTERVIEW ACTUALLY SAID

### 1.1 The Missed Call Problem (said multiple times, different ways)

The owner's phone is on silent while working. This came up repeatedly:

- *"Your phone is switched off. In switch top itself you missed a lot of calls."*
- *"While doing your hair wash you are not picking the calls."*
- *"I call you — you didn't pick up."*

Every missed call is a customer who walked to the next salon. The friend calculated it bluntly:
> *"One month of my money you lost. 15 days of my money you lost."*

That's not a complaint — that's a revenue leak the owner already knows about but has no fix for.

---

### 1.2 The "I Assume You're Busy" Problem (biggest hidden leak)

This is bigger than missed calls. Regulars stop calling because they assume the owner is always occupied:

> *"They have a misconception — you're busy, so who will call you every time?"*

These are referred customers — the most loyal segment. They don't go to a competitor out of preference. They go because they don't know you're available. If the owner could broadcast "I'm free right now," they'd come immediately:

> *"If they know, first day itself — first day evening is free — they will come. They are ready. But they don't know."*

This is free revenue. No acquisition cost. No convincing needed. Just a signal.

---

### 1.3 The Slot Waste Problem

Owner finishes a customer early — 10–15 min slot opens up. Nobody knows. It goes empty.

> *"Sometime things work — he might finish the work speed and in 10 min the slot will be free."*

The friend's solution:
> *"After I am saying outside — you are losing there. You became a slot. You can again call me."*

The owner doesn't call back. Not laziness — he's already on the next customer. **AI does this automatically the moment owner types `done`.**

---

### 1.4 The Service Upsell Problem

Owner never mentions services during the call. Just books what the customer asks for.

> *"While calling you are not asking — haircut or trimming or face wash — you are not asking. That is also one reason."*

First-time customers don't even know what services exist:
> *"My friends don't know you have services — facial, D-tan, coloring — this and all they don't know."*

Every inbound call is an upsell opportunity being missed. A returning customer who gets a haircut every time doesn't know facial is ₹400 and takes 20 min. AI asks every time, naturally.

---

### 1.5 The Relationship Memory Problem

The owner knows every regular by feel — last visit, preference, frequency. But this doesn't scale:

> *"When he last came, you need to be here. When he might come, you need to be here. What he did — everything is in here [his head]. So it is your attack every time."*

For a solo operator this works. For 2+ chairs or 2+ stylists:
> *"Minimum two people — it is difficult to manage the memory. It came last time, when will he come again, why he is not coming — these are all business."*

When a stylist leaves, they take customer relationships with them. The salon loses the customer. AI keeps that relationship at the salon level, not the individual level.

---

### 1.6 The Language Reality

The owner naturally switches language mid-sentence based on who's calling. This is not optional — it's how Bangalore works:

> *"You are talking Hindi, Kannada, English. New customer coming English — you are talking. Tamil customer — I talk Tamil. Nepali also coming. Punjabi also."*

The AI must do exactly this — detect language in the first sentence and respond in kind.

Core 3: **Kannada, Hindi, English**
Extended: Telugu, Tamil, Punjabi, Nepali

---

### 1.7 WhatsApp Reality (overturned assumption)

This was a critical discovery. The friend assumed the owner uses WhatsApp heavily. He doesn't:

> *"WhatsApp is very rare. I am not free. I use it only when I am free."*
> *"Calling checking message delete — this handle very rare WhatsApp."*

The owner's actual workflow: **Voice calls → read SMS → delete**. WhatsApp is a passive glance-when-free channel.

**Product implication:** The AI must lead with voice. WhatsApp is for owner-side notifications (booking alerts, commands when free) — not for customer interactions. Customers call. Owner commands via WhatsApp when he has 30 seconds between cuts.

---

### 1.8 What the Owner Said He Wants from a Demo

> *"Just come, demo. As a real customer. I receive the call — your AI agent. I will see your schedule, schedule and tell, what are the things you want, and it will arrange, waiting list, if anyone got cancel, pull the waiting list, talk in all languages."*

He wants to **experience it as the owner.** Not a video. Not a slide. A live call where AI books a real slot and his WhatsApp buzzes. That's the entire demo.

## PART 2 — CUSTOMER SEGMENTS (Deep)

### 2.1 The Three Customer Types on This Street

The friend laid these out precisely:

| Segment | When they come | Behavior pattern | AI value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Software people** | Sat/Sun, or after 5pm weekdays | Predictable. Ready to pay. OK with Friday/Thursday too. | Schedule them in advance, send reminders |
| **Students** | Random timing | Price-sensitive, styling-focused | Offer packages, upsell coloring/styling |
| **Uncles** | Morning, when shop opens | Routine-driven. Sometimes combine with vegetable shopping or errand nearby. Dad brings son. | Morning slot preference remembered, proactive "morning slots open" message |

> *"These are the fixed. These regularly come. Software people are coming because Saturday Sunday all day they are ready."*

All three segments **go elsewhere if no answer.** None of them call back.

---

### 2.2 Walk-in vs Call-in vs Referred

The interview surfaced three acquisition channels:

**Walk-ins** (street traffic):
> *"Walking customer — first time will be difficult. Board. White dog board. That is all."*

Walk-ins don't need booking. They need queue position and honest wait time. AI handles: "Walk-in just arrived, 20-min wait — shall I add you to queue?"

**Call-ins** (phone bookings):
The primary channel. AI owns this completely.

**Referred regulars** (highest value):
> *"My friends who I referred — they are regular. They don't go outside. They know whenever you are there they will come."*

These people will come — they just need a signal that the slot is open. Re-engagement campaigns target exactly this group.

## PART 3 — SALON TYPES AND HOW AI SCALES

### 3.1 Solo Operator (Raj's model)
- 1 chair, 1 person
- Personal phone = business phone
- No reception, no assistant
- Everything in owner's head
- Revenue: ₹80,000–₹1,50,000/month
- Pain: **Can't answer phone while cutting**
- AI role: Full receptionist replacement

### 3.2 Small Salon (2–3 chairs, unisex)
Unisex salons are booming in Bangalore:
> *"Everyone is putting unisex. Unisex minimum 2-3 chairs. Big thing. Big market."*

- 2–3 stylists, no dedicated reception
- Customers have stylist preferences
- Revenue: ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000/month
- Pain: **Relationship doesn't scale. Stylist leaving = customer leaving.**
- AI role: Per-stylist scheduling + CRM at salon level (not stylist level)

### 3.3 Big Salon (4+ chairs, managed)
> *"Big salon — minimum 3 seats, maximum 20, 15 we can serve. One guy for reception. Manager is there. Targets given to stylists."*

- Stylists get ₹18,000–₹70,000/month salary
- Targets set per stylist
- Digital payments to stylists
- Manager is the main operator
- Pain: **Reception bottleneck, no re-engagement, customer data scattered**
- AI role: Overflow call handling + automated re-engagement + weekly business reports

### 3.4 Spa (Adjacent, not now)
The friend specifically raised this:
> *"Spa — nice business. Take license. Body massage, luxury massage. ₹1,400–₹10,000 range. Boys to boys, girls to girls. Separate section. Need license."*

Spa customers have a distinct behavior:
> *"Spa — people like to talk. One person coming — they don't like [silence]. Personalization is key."*

Same AI core works. Different prompt, different pricing (₹3,499+/month). **Phase 4 expansion target.**

## PART 6 — WHAT THE AI AGENT MUST HANDLE (Complete)

### 6.1 Inbound Call Flows

```
CALL RECEIVED
│
├── Owner is available + slot open
│   └── AI books → confirms → WhatsApp to owner
│
├── Owner is on break
│   └── AI says "Back at [time], earliest slot [time] — shall I book?"
│
├── Owner marked FULL
│   └── AI says "Fully booked today. First slot tomorrow 9am — shall I book?"
│
├── Slot taken, others available
│   └── AI offers next 2–3 slots → customer picks → books
│
├── No slots for rest of day
│   └── AI adds to waitlist → tells customer "We'll call when a slot opens"
│
├── First-time customer
│   └── AI reads services + prices → asks what they need → books
│
├── Returning customer
│   └── AI greets by name → "Last time haircut + beard trim — same today?"
│
├── Customer asks about price
│   └── AI reads price list → offers to book
│
├── Urgent walk-in calling ahead
│   └── AI checks current queue → "About 20 min wait — shall I add you?"
│
└── Customer wants to cancel
    └── AI cancels → immediately calls #1 on waitlist
```

### 6.2 Owner WhatsApp Commands (Complete Set)

| Command | AI Response |
|---|---|
| `done` | "Next: [Name] at [time] ([N] min). Waitlist: [Name] waiting — [phone]" |
| `break 20` | Sets break. Tells callers next slot after [time]. |
| `back` | Resumes booking. Confirms "Taking bookings again." |
| `full` | Blocks day. All callers offered tomorrow 9am. |
| `cancel 4pm` | Cancels slot. Calls waitlist #1 immediately. |
| `schedule` | Sends full today's list as WhatsApp message |
| `waitlist` | Sends waitlist with names + phones |
| `walkin haircut` | Adds walk-in block to current queue |
| `arrived Rahul` | Marks Rahul arrived. Updates queue position. |
| `note Rahul prefers short trim` | Saves to Rahul's profile for next call |
| `free tomorrow morning` | AI broadcasts to top 5 inactive regulars |

### 6.3 Proactive AI Actions (No Owner Input Needed)

| Trigger | AI Does |
|---|---|
| Regular hasn't visited in 14 days | WhatsApp: "Hey [Name], haven't seen you in a while — [Salon] has slots this weekend. Want to book?" |
| Owner has 2+ empty slots tomorrow | Nudge sent to top 3 inactive regulars |
| Customer cancellation received | Immediately calls #1 on waitlist by voice |
| Appointment completed | 30 min later: "Thanks for visiting! Rate your experience 1–5" |
| Rating 4–5 received | "Glad you liked it! Want to book your next visit?" |
| Rating 1–2 received | Alert sent to owner: "[Name] gave 2 stars — might want to follow up" |
| Sunday 8pm | Weekly business summary sent to owner's WhatsApp |

### 6.4 What AI Says on Every Call (Upsell Logic)

```
IF returning customer AND last service was ONLY haircut:
  → "Would you also like a D-tan or facial today? It's ₹400 and adds 20 minutes."

IF first-time customer:
  → Read full menu. "We do haircut ₹200, beard trim ₹100, facial ₹400, D-tan ₹500."

IF booking duration < 30 min AND owner has gap after:
  → "You have a little time — want to add a head massage? Just ₹150 extra."
```
